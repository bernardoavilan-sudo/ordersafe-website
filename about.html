About OrderSafe

OrderSafe was founded on a simple but critical observation: legacy fraud prevention tools are fundamentally blind to the modern cybercriminal playbook.

While major payment processors focus their resources on surface-level checks (like AVS and basic IP blacklists), organized carding rings have evolved. They now use sophisticated infrastructure like Virtual Machines, Anti-Detect browsers, and residential proxy networks to bypass traditional defenses.

"We don't just look at the transaction. We look at the attacker's environment."

Our team combines expertise in offensive cybersecurity, AI engineering, and payment risk. We built the FraudShield Engine by monitoring deep-dark-web forums, analyzing Non-VBV lists, and reverse-engineering the techniques used by professional carders.

Today, OrderSafe provides a robust, explainable, and API-first solution designed to be seamlessly integrated into payment gateways and large-scale e-commerce platforms. Our mission is to democratize enterprise-grade security and stop fraud before it happens.
