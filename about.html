About OrderSafe

OrderSafe was born from a simple observation: traditional fraud prevention tools are blind to modern cybercriminal techniques.

While payment processors only look at the surface of a transaction, we use a 110-rule offensive intelligence engine to detect advanced threats like anti-detect browsers, non-VBV cards, and account takeovers.

Our mission is to democratize enterprise-grade security for SMBs and platforms worldwide.
